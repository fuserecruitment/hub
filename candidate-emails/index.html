How it works today

The current flows, step by step

Every email in send order, with its subject, topic, link and results. Topic clicks per 1,000 sent is the fairest way to compare emails. Click and open rates exist only for A/B-tested emails. Open "More" on any email for its summary, A/B variants and every link.

Proposed for the rebuild

One flow per journey stage

Each stage starts from a Bullhorn event and ends by handing the candidate to the next stage. Steps reuse the best existing emails where they fit, add articles already on the Fuse blog that no email uses, and mark where new content is needed. Placed has two flows, on-hire and permanent, built on the two placement series running today. Not looking (Passive) has no event to react to, so it's split into three tiers by how engaged people are: the more they click, the more often they hear from Fuse. Check-ins move people back into the journey.

Questions to decide

ContentFor the content team

  1. Which journey stages get their own workflow: applied, interview, offer, placed? Which Bullhorn status or event starts each one?
  2. The Placed stage has no live content, and the retired workplace articles got few clicks from a Passive audience. Write new content, or test the retired pieces on newly placed candidates first?
  3. Refresh the top performers that are dated (the 2024 checklist, the 2023 interview prep and salary articles) before reusing them?
  4. Keep job picks in stage emails? They earn a third of Active clicks.
  5. Keep the Prime Super emails? They get the fewest clicks in either workflow. Are they sponsored or contractual?
  6. Test bigger changes than subject lines? All 22 Active A/B tests finished within 2 points of each other.

Logic and dataFor Nicole

  1. Can Bullhorn submission and placement statuses trigger stage workflows directly (New Lead, Client Submission, Interview Scheduled, Offer, Placed)?
  2. Should both workflows remove candidates who stop matching, instead of pausing them?
  3. Which automation owns Active → Passive (Day 173 step or No App L6M), and which owns Passive → Active (62899 or 83616)?
  4. Was pausing "Update Passive/Placed/Unavailable to ACTIVE" in June intentional?
  5. Should Passive also exclude the Candidate Marketing Exclusion List?
