Where the problems are

Five themes account for almost all of the feedback. Counts show how many respondents raised each one.

24mentions

Missing or incorrect information

Top table not populated from screening notes, old CV used, dates misplaced, irrelevant tickets, CVs with tables not parsing.

I have to also copy and paste the most current CV in, as usually it takes the old one.
19mentions

Formatting shifts on download

Font sizes, spacing, margins, header borders and bullets change between preview and file. Word export is largely avoided.

The preview will look perfect, then I save and download it.
15mentions

Generic content

Summaries draw on the CV, not screening notes. Not tailored to the role. Enhance is unpredictable.

I've had a few clients say the profiles are clearly AI generated.
10mentions

No editing once saved

Small changes mean regenerating the whole profile. Undo resets all edits. Files renamed by hand every time.

If you Ctrl+Z it takes you right back to the beginning.
9mentions

Reliability and loading

Clearing cache and logging back into Bullhorn to get Present to load. Slow generation and broken templates.

Half the time I have to exit out and clear cache.

A divided view on rewriting CVs

Most of the team

Want Present to write stronger, more tailored content from screening notes and the job brief.

A smaller group, plus clients such as AdvanCell

Want the candidate's own CV kept as is, with Fuse branding added and contact details removed.

Both needs are valid. Offering two distinct templates resolves the tension.

Three common workarounds

The rewrite loop

Most common, around 10 people

  1. Generate in Present
  2. Copy a section out
  3. Rewrite in ChatGPT
  4. Strip formatting
  5. Paste back and refix

The format rescue

3 people, including the 30+ minute response

  1. Generate in Present
  2. Download as PDF
  3. Convert in iLovePDF
  4. Fix spacing, fonts, bullets in Word
  5. Send to client

The bypass

Around 7 people, at least some of the time

  1. Feed CV, notes and brief to ChatGPT
  2. Output into a blank Fuse template
  3. Use Present only for work history
  4. Check and send

What this tells us

No one who stays within Present takes more than 10 minutes. Every 10 to 20 minute response comes from someone using external AI, and the one 30+ minute response relies on a PDF converter.

External tools are fixing gaps in Present, not replacing it. Each round trip adds copying, reformatting and checking. Closing the content and formatting gaps would bring most of this time back.

Risks and opportunities

Risks
  • CVs, interview transcripts and salary details are going into personal AI accounts. Only one respondent mentioned removing names first.
  • Candidate profiles are uploaded to free PDF conversion websites.
  • Client-facing profiles vary in quality and style depending on who built them.
  • The best prompts sit in individual accounts, not with the team.
Opportunities
  • The team's prompts show exactly what a good profile looks like. They can shape Present's backend prompts.
  • Six people already use Amplify chat and their own prompts inside the Fuse ecosystem, a safer alternative worth scaling.
  • One approved tool and a shared prompt library would standardise output quickly.
